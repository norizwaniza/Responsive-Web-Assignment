Ada Lovelace

The world's first computer programmer

The Legacy of the Enchantress of Numbers

  • 1815 - Born in London to Lord Byron and Lady Byron.
  • 1833 - Met Charles Babbage, the "father of computers," and became interested in his Difference Engine.
  • 1842 - Translated an article on Babbage's Analytical Engine and added her own extensive "Notes."
  • 1843 - Published Note G, which contained the first computer program (an algorithm to compute Bernoulli numbers).
  • 1852 - Passed away at the age of 36, leaving behind a vision of computers as more than just calculators.

"That brain of mine is something more than merely mortal; as time will show."

-- Ada Lovelace
